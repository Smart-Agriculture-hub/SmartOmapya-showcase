Smart Omapya

Exploring technology-driven solutions for agriculture

Smart Omapya is a digital agriculture platform currently in development. The project explores how technology can support farmers and Agricultural Science learners through accessible digital tools and learning resources.

Project Vision
The vision of Smart Omapya is to bring agriculture and technology closer together by exploring practical digital solutions for agricultural information, learning and farm-related activities.

Planned Features
- Farmer Support: Digital resources designed to support farmers with agricultural information.
- Agricultural Science Learning: Learning materials and quizzes for students.
- Crop and Weed Detection: Exploring technology-assisted crop identification and weed detection.
- Research Hub: A planned space for agricultural research and educational resources.
- Digital Learning Tools: Exploring ways to make agricultural knowledge easier to access.

Technologies
The project explores web development technologies, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js and database integration.

The final technology stack and features may change as development progresses.

Development Status
Status: Work in Progress

Smart Omapya is an ongoing personal development project. Features are still being developed, tested and improved. The planned features listed above are not necessarily fully implemented.

About the Developer
I am an Agricultural Science and Computer Science education student interested in the intersection of agriculture, education and information technology. Through Smart Omapya, I aim to explore how digital tools can contribute to agricultural learning and practical problem-solving.

Project Goals
- Explore the use of digital technology in agriculture.
- Support agricultural learning through digital resources.
- Develop practical web development and database skills.
- Continue improving the platform through learning, testing and experimentation.
